# Concierge booking

Context: Massage Club, Madrid. 1 onboarded studio (Calma Madrid Spa). Concierge-first: Jordan books real people by hand to learn before automating. A reimbursed test booking is a paid experiment, not revenue.

## Mode A: Invite (before the booking)
1. Ask (if missing): who is the person, how Jordan knows them, language (EN/ES), budget for this test.
2. Write the WhatsApp invite in their language:
   - 3 short lines max
   - Personal, not marketing
   - One concrete offer: a day, a time window, the studio
   - Easy yes: "Reply yes and I'll book it"
3. Give 2 variants (warm / direct). Jordan picks and sends. Never send for him.
4. BOARD: "Send invite to [name]" plus "Book slot at Calma once they reply".

## Mode B: Debrief (after the booking)
1. Ask Jordan 5 questions, one message:
   - Did they book? If not, where did it stall?
   - What did they say before and after?
   - Would they pay full price next time? Their words.
   - What surprised you?
   - Cost to Massage Club (reimbursement)?
2. Write a log entry:
   Date | Person (first name) | Booked Y/N | Paid / reimbursed | Quote | Lesson | Next experiment
3. Compare with earlier entries if Jordan pastes the log: what pattern is forming?
4. Recommend the single next experiment.

## Never
- Count a reimbursed booking as revenue.
- Claim more studios or partners than exist.
- Suggest building product features unless 5+ bookings show the need.
