## What to do with each outcome

| They say | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Yes | Confirm with the studio, then set the row to `confirmed` and create the booking |
| A different time | Take it back to the studio same day. Do not leave it open overnight |
| No / no reply in 48h | Set the row to `dismissed` with a reason. A dead lead in the queue is worse than no lead |

**Set the stage on every one of the 13, including the nos.** The reason these went cold is that
nothing forced a status, so they sat looking alive.

## The number that matters

Not replies, not bookings. **Completed sessions.** All-time this business is 41 booked, 17
cancelled, 3 completed. Five confirmed bookings that nobody attends is the same as today.
